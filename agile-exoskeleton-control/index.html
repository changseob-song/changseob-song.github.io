Project overview

Human motion priors meet physics-based locomotion learning.

We explore agile lower-limb exoskeleton control through human motion priors and musculoskeletal simulation. An adversarial motion prior learned from a small set of reference motion clips supports diverse locomotor behaviors across uneven terrain.

The project brings together learning from human movement and physically grounded policy training. The videos below demonstrate walking and running in musculoskeletal simulation. Detailed methods and quantitative evaluation will be added as they become available.
